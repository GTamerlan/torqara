The team

Vyper Space is building contactless detumbling for low-Earth orbit, starting with the Arago V1.1 ground-test arm.

Founders

CO-FOUNDER

Brandon Chang

Harvard University · Applied Mathematics & Computer Science

Brandon works on orbital modeling and flight software. At UC Santa Barbara’s Yeung Group he built ODEM-S, a nonlinear dynamical model that forecasts low-Earth-orbit debris growth through 2050 using coupled differential equations, stochastic collision probabilities and time-varying launch rates, calibrated against roughly 12,000 satellite launches.

He was principal scientist and software lead for payloads on NASA’s High Altitude Student Platform and RockSat-X, leading six developers across downlink, sensor integration and post-flight analysis. That work included a custom telemetry protocol and Python data pipeline for a 10-channel Geiger-counter payload. With the Colorado Space Grant Consortium he programmed the sensor suite of a LunaSat bound for the lunar surface.

Before Vyper he founded Hubble, an academic collaboration platform with more than 5,600 monthly users, and Shaman, a model-based forecasting engine.

  • Orbital debris modeling
  • Flight software
  • Telemetry & data pipelines

CO-FOUNDER

Tamerlan Goglichidze

Tamerlan builds hardware and robotics. He designed and built the Pi Board, a Raspberry Pi–controlled robotic chessboard in which a stepper-driven XY mechanism moves magnets beneath the board to slide the pieces, with custom software that tracks every piece across the 8×8 grid. The project was featured by Raspberry Pi Official Magazine and Hackaday.

He is also a FIDE-rated chess player.
