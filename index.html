The concept

Torqara explores an arm-mounted, motor-driven permanent magnet to influence the rotation of selected electrically conductive targets without touching them.

The concept combines observation, arm positioning, and a commanded magnetic field. Each is a separate engineering problem in a feedback loop.

Detumbling does not remove an object from orbit. A capture and removal mission requires additional systems and operations.

The engineering questions

THE DETAILS
MATTER.

What can respond to the field?

Aluminum and copper are candidates for target-specific analysis. Mixed structures depend on conductive paths and geometry. Nonconductive glass, plastics, and rock are not directly suited to this eddy-current mechanism.

Does any magnet rotation cause braking?

No. Material, geometry, separation, relative rotation, and the commanded field determine the response. Arbitrary magnet rotation can add rotational energy. A controller must choose and verify its action.

What does this demonstration establish?

The website presents a research concept and an illustrative user-provided model. It is not a validated satellite dynamics simulation or measured flight result. It makes no guaranteed stopping-time or efficacy claim.

Spacecraft reaction and thermal effects are not modeled here. The engineering explorer explains the model and its limits.
