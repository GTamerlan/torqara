From concept to evidence

The floating experiment, zero-g laboratory, rocket and orbital mission are imagined staging. The arm is proposed ground-test CAD. No hardware, completed experiment or launch is claimed.

Braking is illustrative and depends on relative motion, target material, geometry and control. Arbitrary magnet rotation does not always brake. No measured performance or physical stopping time is shown.

Spacecraft reaction and thermal effects are not modeled. Detumbling does not remove an object from orbit.
