Privacy & analytics

Updated November 9, 2026.

Optional website analytics

Vyper Space uses Umami Cloud to understand use of this website. The optional analytics script loads only if you choose “Allow analytics.” “No thanks” keeps it off. You can change your choice using “Analytics choices” in the footer. We also honor browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by not loading this analytics script.

When enabled, analytics reports website page paths, visit times, referring website origins, approximate country/city and basic browser/device information. Umami processes network information, including IP addresses, to provide its service and derive approximate location. We do not send names, email addresses, form contents, passwords or a persistent identity to Umami, and do not enable session replay, screen recording or advertising tracking.

Our code removes URL fragments and arbitrary query parameters from analytics payloads and strips referrer paths and queries. Opaque outreach campaign codes may be retained to compare link activity. A visit associated with a campaign link does not establish who opened it: links can be forwarded and automated scanners may open them.

Your choice and retention

Your browser stores your yes/no preference locally under “vyper-analytics-consent-v1.” This preference is not an analytics identifier. Umami's Hobby plan retains analytics for up to six months. The account uses Umami's United States data region. The dashboard is restricted to the account owner and people authorized to use that account; no public analytics sharing link is enabled.

See Umami's privacy policy for its processing practices. Consent choices affect this optional analytics script, not ordinary web-hosting requests or security logs.

Hosting and embedded documents

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles ordinary web requests under GitHub's privacy statement. The whitepaper page embeds a Google document. Google handles requests to that embedded document under Google's privacy policy. The Vyper analytics choice does not control those services.

A “whitepaper opened” analytics event means the website's whitepaper page is unlocked and visible. It does not establish that the document was read, how far someone scrolled in Google's viewer, or whether someone opened a direct Google Docs link.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact founders@vyperspace.com. If you decline optional analytics, the website and whitepaper remain available with their existing access requirements.
