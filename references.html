01 / Source register

The work behind the idea.

Five selected primary sources.
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

A selected reading list, not an exhaustive prior-art or patent review. No novelty, priority, or uniqueness claim is made.

  1. 01
    2021Peer-reviewed researchNature

    Dexterous magnetic manipulation of conductive non-magnetic objects

    L. N. Pham, G. F. Tabor, A. Pourkand, J. L. B. Aman, T. Hermans & J. J. Abbott. Nature 598, 439–443 (2021). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03966-6.

    What it shows
    Rotating dipole fields can manipulate conductive objects. The paper distinguishes six-degree-of-freedom numerical demonstrations from three-degree-of-freedom physical experiments.
    Claim boundary
    The work focuses on sphere-based models and controlled experiments. It does not demonstrate arbitrary-spacecraft detumbling or orbital capture.
    Read Pham et al. Paper + supplement / PDF↗
  2. 02
    2024Experimental detumblingIEEE iSpaRo

    Characterization of a Rotating Magnetic Dipole Field for Contactless Detumbling of Space Debris

    T. J. Allen, A. J. Sperry, N. R. Posselli, B. A. Minor & J. J. Abbott. 2024 International Conference on Space Robotics (iSpaRo), 119–124. DOI: 10.1109/iSpaRo60631.2024.10687552.

    What it shows
    A motor-driven permanent magnet was tested beside an aluminum sphere on a one-axis rig, comparing rotating and static fields. The paper also estimates behavior after accounting for rig friction.
    Claim boundary
    A one-axis laboratory result is not a free-flying three-axis satellite demonstration. The authors caution that approximately stationary-target wrench models may be insufficient for tumbling targets.
    Read Allen et al. Conference paper / PDF↗
  3. 03
    2025Modeling & controlIJRR

    Magnetic manipulation of unknown and complex conductive nonmagnetic objects with application in the remediation of space debris

    G. F. Tabor, L. N. Pham, J. J. Abbott & T. Hermans. The International Journal of Robotics Research 44(7), 1117–1137 (2025). DOI: 10.1177/02783649241300167.

    What it shows
    A fitted wrench model and adaptive control for conductive-object manipulation, with physical demonstrations involving copper and aluminum targets and selected nonspherical objects.
    Claim boundary
    The experiments concern slowly moving objects. Rapid target rotation and source reaction effects remain limitations; these fitted results cannot be transferred blindly to a tumbling spacecraft.
    Read Tabor et al. Journal article / PDF↗
  4. 04
    23 April 2024Agency / industry contextESA

    ESA advances Clearspace-1 development

    European Space Agency. Space Safety, 23 April 2024. Agency development announcement.

    What it describes
    Development of a rendezvous, capture, and atmospheric-reentry mission, with an industrial team led by OHB and ClearSpace responsible for close-proximity and capture operations.
    Claim boundary
    This dated announcement describes mission development, not completed debris removal or a magnetic-detumbling demonstration. These organizations are cited as sector context, not Torqara customers or partners.
    Read the ESA announcement Primary agency source↗
  5. 05
    20 August 2024Agency / industry contextJAXA

    JAXA concludes partnership-type contract for Phase II of its Commercial Removal of Debris Demonstration (CRD2)

    Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency. Press release, 20 August 2024.

    What it describes
    JAXA reports Phase I rendezvous/proximity demonstrations and debris imagery, and a contract with Astroscale Japan for a subsequent removal-technology demonstration.
    Claim boundary
    Inspection and proximity operations are distinct from capture and removal. The release does not establish Torqara capability or a relationship between Torqara and either organization.
    Read the JAXA announcement Primary agency source↗

02 / Material scope

Conductivity
is a condition.

Eddy-current coupling requires an electrically conductive path. This is a material and geometry question, not a blanket claim about space debris. [1] [3]

How to read the concept’s material scope
TargetBoundary
Aluminum & copperConductive, nonmagnetic materials studied in the cited work. Their presence alone does not establish useful control authority for a particular target.
Nonconductive glass, plastics & rockOutside this eddy-current mechanism. Conductive coatings or inclusions require separate assessment; the label “debris” says nothing about conductivity.
Mixed-material objectsConditional. Conductive components, their geometry and continuity, relative motion, and source distance must be assessed. A sphere result is not a whole-object qualification.

This scope is an implication of the electrical-conduction requirement, not a validated target-selection catalog. Other magnetic interactions and materials require their own models.

03 / Development boundary

A concept to examine.
A system still to validate.

Torqara is exploring how observation, robotic positioning, and a motor-driven magnet might work together for selected conductive targets. Hardware characterization, target-specific electromagnetic analysis, and integrated testing remain development work.

Detumbling addresses rotation. Capture, servicing, and deorbiting require additional capabilities. The browser model uses scaled rigid-body dynamics and a simplified drag law. Its synthetic camera, geometric arm, and model run times are not measured hardware evidence. Spacecraft reaction, thermal evolution, and orbital dynamics are not modeled.
